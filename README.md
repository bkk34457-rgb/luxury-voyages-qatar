# Luxury Voyages Qatar

A mobile-first showcase website for Luxury Voyages Qatar, a company offering private desert safaris, yacht charters and dhow cruises in and around Doha.

## Sections

- **Hero**: dhows in Doha Bay at golden hour under a deep navy gradient, with a gold call-to-action button
- **Signature experiences**: Inland Sea desert safari, private yacht charter at The Pearl, skyline dhow at dusk, dunes and desert camp
- **The heart of Qatar**: the desert as the highlight of Qatar, with six activities over a golden-hour dune background
- **How it works**: three steps from enquiry to voyage
- **Enquire**: an enquiry form (front end only; not connected to a mail service yet)
- **Footer**: opening hours in Qatar time (GMT+3), with a live Doha clock that shows open or closed

## Design

- Colours: deep navy base, muted gold accents, soft cream text areas
- Type: Barlow Condensed (headings), Instrument Serif (italic accents), Figtree (body), loaded from Google Fonts
- Text animates in on load and on scroll, and the animation is skipped for visitors who turn on reduced motion

## Run it

It's a static site with no build step. Open `index.html` in a browser, or turn on GitHub Pages for this repository (Settings → Pages → deploy from the `main` branch).
