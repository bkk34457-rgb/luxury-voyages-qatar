Out in the desert

South of Doha the dunes run for kilometres down to Khor Al Adaid, where the sand meets the sea. Most of our trips head this way, and it's the part of Qatar guests usually remember best.

  • Dune bashing

    Ride the high dunes near Sealine in a private 4×4 with a licensed desert driver.

  • Inland Sea crossing

    Drive over the dunes to Khor Al Adaid and swim where the desert meets the Gulf.

  • Sandboarding

    Board down steep dune faces, with a guide and kit sized for adults and children.

  • Camel trekking

    A slow ride across the sand at golden hour, led by local handlers.

  • Quad biking

    Self-drive quads on marked dune trails, with a guide riding alongside.

  • Stargazing camp

    Arabic coffee, a fire and a dinner under clear desert skies, far from city lights.
